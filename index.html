RGB Permutation Filter

How does it work?

We define a fixed permutation of the numbers 0–255 once and keep it unchanged forever. When you upload an image, every pixel’s color channels are transformed like this:

  • New R = perm[old R]
  • New G = perm[old G]
  • New B = perm[old B]
  • Alpha (transparency) is kept unchanged

Everything runs locally in your browser — the image is not uploaded to any server.
